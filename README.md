# What I Look for in Product Reviews Before Spending My Money

A short article about how I evaluate product reviews before making an online purchase.

## About

Product ratings don't always tell the complete story. This article explains the things I look for when reading customer reviews, including negative feedback, detailed experiences, recent reviews, recurring problems, and whether the product offers good value for its price.

## Key Topics

- Reading product reviews before purchasing
- Checking negative reviews
- Looking for detailed customer experiences
- Checking recent reviews
- Identifying patterns across reviews
- Comparing product quality with price
- Avoiding disappointing purchases

## Article

**What I Look for in Product Reviews Before Spending My Money**

The article discusses a simple review-first approach to online shopping and explains why looking beyond star ratings can help make more informed purchasing decisions.
